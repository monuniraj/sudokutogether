About SudokuSync

Platform: Web & Android App | Status: Active & Free to Play

SudokuSync (https://sudoku-together-mode.web.app) is a modern, high-performance logic puzzle and brain-training platform designed for solo solvers and competitive friends alike.

Our mission is to elevate classic paper-and-pencil Sudoku into an engaging digital multiplayer experience. Powered by deterministic seed generation (Mulberry32 PRNG), custom mistake limits, real-time board synchronization, and procedural sound synthesis, our platform brings players together on identical, mathematically verified 1-solution puzzles without heavy data transmission.

Key Features & Architecture

  • Deterministic Seeded Generation: Play identical puzzles with friends across web and Android mobile by simply sharing a room link or numeric seed.
  • Scrapbook Design Aesthetic: Premium paper-and-ink visual theme with customizable sticky notes, washi tape, and clean tactile controls.
  • Procedural Web Audio Engine: Dynamic, zero-latency synthesizer sounds created natively via the browser Web Audio API.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Full gameplay capability offline with automatic Firestore synchronization when back online.
100% Free & Transparent:

This application is completely free to play, supported by Google AdSense advertisements, and built with user privacy, speed, and accessibility at its core.
