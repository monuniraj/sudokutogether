About SudokuSync

Publisher: Mo Logic | Version: 1.0.3 | Platform: Web & Google Play Android (com.mologic.sudokusync)

SudokuSync (https://sudokusync.com) is developed and published independently by Mo Logic as a calm, fast-loading classic 9x9 logic puzzle platform built for solo solvers and same-board multiplayer rivals.

Deterministic 9x9 Puzzle Engine & Verified Clue Calibration

Every puzzle is deterministically seeded with Mulberry32 PRNG and validated by our human-logic difficulty grader and MRV bitmask solver so every classic 9x9 grid guarantees a single mathematical solution:

  • Easy (51 clues): Clean row, column, and 3x3 box Singles for smooth daily warm-ups.
  • Medium (39 clues): Balanced multi-step logical intersections.
  • Hard (30 clues): Candidate notes, pairs, pointing reductions, and optional Auto-Notes support.
  • Expert (25 clues): Deep candidate chains, X-Wing patterns, and minimal given digits.

Key Features & Architecture

  • Same-Board Live & Asynchronous Multiplayer: Compete on the exact same 9x9 board using 6-digit numeric Room Codes, optional PIN protection, and direct ?room= browser share links, alongside Recent Players and persistent Google Sign-In Friends lists.
  • Borderless Pastel UI with Light & Dark Themes: Smooth curved cards, soft visual hierarchy, distraction-free grid clarity, and full accessibility across desktop, mobile web, and Android.
  • 7 Verified Smart Assistants: Customize solving with Highlight Identical Numbers, Show Remaining Numbers, Highlight Related Area, Auto-Switch Completed Numbers, Auto-Remove Notes, Auto-Notes (Hard and Expert only), and Fast Fill (Number-First).
  • Procedural Web Audio & Offline Persistence: Zero-latency synthesized sound feedback plus isolated offline storage that syncs seamlessly with Cloud Firestore when signed in.
100% Free & Privacy-First:

SudokuSync is free to play across Web and Android, supported transparently by Google AdSense and Google AdMob (with Google UMP privacy controls in applicable regions).
